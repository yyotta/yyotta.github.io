## RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

<div class="entry">

    <span class="entry-title">Research on Multi-Step Reasoning Methods for Complicated Visual Problems under Data Constraints</span>
  </div>
  <div class="entry-head">

    <span class="entry-time">Jan. 2026 - Dec. 2029</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="entry-list">
    <li>Led the continual adaptation direction for data-constrained multi-step visual reasoning, studying how multimodal models can acquire new knowledge without eroding prior reasoning capabilities.</li>
    <li>Designed risk-aware orthogonal knowledge-subspace updates that isolate new edits from previously learned subspaces, protecting stability-critical knowledge during long update sequences.</li>
    <li>Developed multi-granularity knowledge integration and semantic activation mechanisms to retrieve edited knowledge selectively while preserving model behavior on unrelated inputs.</li>
    <li>This work was accepted as a <span class="hot">Spotlight</span> at <strong>NeurIPS 2026</strong>.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="entry">

    <span class="entry-time">May 2025 - Jul. 2026</span>
  </div>
  <ul class="entry-list">
    <li>Developed KSS-MoE for continual visual instruction tuning, using internal activations to fuse task-relevant low-rank subspaces from specialized experts for fine-grained collaboration at inference.</li>

    <li>Evaluated the framework across all eight CoIN tasks, achieving state-of-the-art continual-learning performance; the work was published at <strong>AAAI 2026</strong>.</li>
  </ul>

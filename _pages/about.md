## RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

**Research on Multi-Step Reasoning Methods for Complicated Visual Problems under Data Constraints**<br>
*Graduate Researcher* | NSFC General Program; Jan. 2026 - Dec. 2029

- Led the continual adaptation direction for data-constrained multi-step visual reasoning, studying how multimodal models can acquire new knowledge without eroding prior reasoning capabilities.
- Designed risk-aware orthogonal knowledge-subspace updates that isolate new edits from previously learned subspaces, protecting stability-critical knowledge during long update sequences.
- Developed multi-granularity knowledge integration and semantic activation mechanisms to retrieve edited knowledge selectively while preserving model behavior on unrelated inputs.

**Step-Level DPO for MoE LLMs in Continual Learning of Complex Multi-Domain Tasks**<br>
*Lead Graduate Researcher* | Xiaomi Open-Competition Research Program; May 2025 - Jul. 2026

- Developed KSS-MoE for continual visual instruction tuning, using internal activations to fuse task-relevant low-rank subspaces from specialized experts for fine-grained collaboration at inference.
- Designed specialized and shared expert collaboration with conflict masks and orthogonal gradient projection to facilitate cross-task transfer while reducing negative interference.
- Evaluated the framework across all eight CoIN tasks, achieving state-of-the-art continual-learning performance; the work was published at **AAAI 2026**.

**Multimodal Intelligent Continual Question Answering with Dynamic-KG RAG**<br>
*Graduate Researcher* | CCF-Zhipu Large Model Innovation Fund; Nov. 2024 - Nov. 2025

- Investigated coordinated adaptation between evolving structured graph knowledge and multimodal model knowledge for temporal and cross-domain learning.
- Constructed visual, textual, multimodal, and shared expert subspaces to strengthen cross-domain knowledge transfer while retaining previously acquired knowledge.
- Introduced cross-subspace orthogonality constraints to decouple domain-specific and general knowledge optimization, improving generalization while mitigating forgetting and conflicts.
